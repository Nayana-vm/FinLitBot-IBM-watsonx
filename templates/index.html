Emergency Cyber Helpline 1930

Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting System:

If money was unauthorizedly debited from your account, act immediately:

  1. Dial 1930 toll-free from your registered mobile number.
  2. Have your Bank Account Number, UPI Transaction ID, and debit SMS timestamp ready.
  3. The dispatch officer immediately alerts both sender and recipient banks to lock funds in the Golden Hour.
  4. File the written incident acknowledgement at cybercrime.gov.in.
